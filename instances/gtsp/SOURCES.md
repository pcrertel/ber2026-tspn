# Where these GTSP instances come from

The 235 files here are redistributed unchanged from two public benchmark
libraries for the Generalized Traveling Salesman Problem: 45 instances of the
GTSP Lib [\[1\]](../../README.md#instance-references) under `gtsplib/` and 190
of the MOM Lib [\[2\]](../../README.md#instance-references) under `momlib/`.
They are the input of `conversion/`.

The lists under `selected-instances/` name the files the conversion reads: every
instance of the two libraries with at most 100 clusters that gives the
coordinates of its points, which the conversion needs.
